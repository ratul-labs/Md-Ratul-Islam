The idea. A refill-based ecosystem: brands supply products to refill stations, customers carry reusable bottles identified with a QR system, and every refill replaces a piece of single-use plastic.

What makes it a system. Physical stations, connected IoT hardware and a digital platform work together, so the loop from brand to customer and back can be tracked and improved.
